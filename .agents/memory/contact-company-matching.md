---
name: Contact company matching
description: Avoid ambiguous company-name matches during LeadOcean enrichment.
---
Match contacts against verified company domains and current employment, not free-text company names or profile headlines.

**Why:** Name searches for the demo companies returned plausible contacts whose employer could not reliably be identified as the requested business. Official websites provided an unambiguous matching boundary; some brands have multiple verified domain aliases.

**How to apply:** Confirm domain aliases from official sites before enrichment. Retain labeled samples when there is no current-employer match rather than presenting an uncertain contact as real.
